# About the Authors

**Antoni Oliver** is an Associate Professor at the Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC), where he directs the Master’s Degree in Translation and Technologies and leads the GGLiC research group. Holding a PhD in Linguistics and a background in Telecommunications Engineering, his research focuses on Machine Translation, Computational Linguistics, terminology extraction, and natural language processing. He principal-investigates major competitive research projects, including **TAN-IBE**, **TamTAS** (Terminology-Aware MT for Accessible Science), and research on LLMs for Iberian Romance languages. He has authored numerous open-source tools for translation and parallel corpus creation, such as *PosEdiOn* and *LitPC*. His research is widely published in high-impact international journals such as *Language Resources and Evaluation*, *Procesamiento del Lenguaje Natural*, *Tradumàtica*, and *Linguamàtica*, as well as top computational linguistics conferences like EAMT, EACL, and the Machine Translation Summit.  
**Contact:** aoliverg@uoc.edu

**Sergi Alvarez Vidal** is a researcher and lecturer specializing in Translation Technology, Computational Linguistics, and Natural Language Processing[cite: 1]. His primary research focuses on the empirical evaluation of Machine Translation (MT)—including Neural MT and Large Language Models—and its integration into professional workflows, with a strong emphasis on low-resource languages like Catalan and Aranese[cite: 1]. He actively participates in competitive research projects, including **TAN-IBE**, **TamTAS**, and **Generatese2LT**[cite: 1]. His work is published in journals such as *Mutatis Mutandis*, *Linguamàtica*, *Ampersand*, *Language and Law / Linguagem E Direito*, and *Revista Tradumàtica*[cite: 1]. Alongside academic publishing, he engages in industry consultancy and open-source tool development, including the *PosEdiOn* post-editing framework[cite: 1]. In higher education, Sergi teaches translation technologies, localization, and terminology, leading innovation projects like the "Virtual Translation Agency" and serving as Associate Editor for *Cogent Arts & Humanities*[cite: 1].  
**Contact:** salvarezvidal@uoc.edu
